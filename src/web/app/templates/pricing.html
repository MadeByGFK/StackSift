FAQ

How many credits does a PDF export cost?

2 credits per report.

Does re-downloading a PDF cost credits?

No. The 2-credit charge applies only when a PDF is first generated for a report. Re-downloading the same export within its 7-day window is free.

Do I need an account to use StackSift?

No. The free tier works without signup — just drop in a log file and get your incident summary. Accounts are only needed for premium features.

What are credits?

Credits unlock premium capabilities like PDF export. Credits are deducted per premium operation, only after a successful operation. Credits never expire.

What happens if my analysis fails?

You are never charged for failed analyses. Credits are only deducted after a successful operation. If something goes wrong on our end, your credit is restored automatically.

How long are my results available?

Generated reports remain accessible for 7 days after creation. Your credits themselves never expire.

Can I get a refund?

All sales are final. Credit purchases are non-refundable, subject to applicable legal obligations.
