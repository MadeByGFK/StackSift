FAQ

What does the Investigation Report include?

The Investigation Report adds an Investigation Brief — the investigation lead, the observed sequence, and prioritized checks — ahead of the full per-issue analysis of your log. It costs $9 per report, paid by card at checkout. Exports (PDF, CSV, JSON) are included free, branding is included, and the report is kept for 90 days. Payment is captured at checkout; if generation fails, your purchase is never consumed — the entitlement stays available and you can retry. A purchase that can never be redeemed is refundable on request via disputes@goodfolkdigital.com. See a sample Investigation Report →

How much does a PDF export cost?

Nothing. Exports are free for all reports: PDF for every report, and CSV/JSON for single-file reports (batch and comparison reports export as PDF). Re-downloading an export is free too.

How much does an oversize analysis cost?

$5 per run (Large-File Analysis), paid at the quote step. Oversize covers files up to 100 MB / 500,000 lines; files within the free limits (10 MB / 50,000 lines) stay free.

How much does a batch analysis cost?

$5 flat. Batch analysis combines 2–5 files into one report for a single $5 price — not per file. Exporting the combined report as a PDF is free.

How much does a comparison cost?

$5 flat, paid at the quote step — even if the two logs are identical or show no material differences. Exporting the comparison report as a PDF is free.

What happened to credits?

Credits are retired. StackSift no longer sells credit packs, new accounts no longer receive signup credits, and nothing is priced in credits any more. Paid features are bought per report in dollars, as listed above.

What happens if my analysis fails?

You are never charged for a failed generation. Payment is captured at checkout; if generation fails, your purchase is never consumed — the entitlement stays available and you can retry.

How long are my results available?

Free reports remain accessible for 7 days after creation. Paid reports and paid runs (Investigation Report, Compare, Batch, Large-File Analysis) are kept for 90 days. Retention extensions are no longer sold.

Can I get a refund?

A purchase that can never be redeemed is refundable on request via disputes@goodfolkdigital.com. Otherwise, per-report purchases are final, subject to applicable legal obligations.

Do I need an account to use StackSift?

No. The free tier works without signup — just drop in a log file and get your incident summary. Accounts are only needed for paid reports and your report history.
