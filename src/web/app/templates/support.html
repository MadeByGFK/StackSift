Support

How to get help with StackSift.

Before reporting

  • Read the message on the page — expired reports (older than 7 days) and oversized uploads (over 10 MB / 50,000 lines) explain most errors.
  • Retrying a failed analysis never costs you a credit: credits are deducted only after a successful operation.

Report a bug

  • Open an issue at github.com/MadeByGFK/StackSift/issues.
  • Include: what you were doing, the exact error message, the file type (plain text, JSON-lines), and the browser or CLI version.
  • Please redact secrets yourself before sharing any log excerpt — automatic redaction is best effort and can miss things.

Billing

  • Credit purchases are not enabled yet — the credits page describes how they will work at launch.
  • All credit sales will be final and non-refundable, subject to applicable legal obligations. If you are ever charged in error, open a GitHub issue with your account email and it will be reviewed manually.
