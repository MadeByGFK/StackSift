Terms

Plain-language terms. StackSift is a Goodfolk Digital project.

The service

  • StackSift analyzes log files you upload and returns an incident summary: severity counts, recurring issues, a health verdict, and what to investigate first.
  • The free tier needs no account: 10 analyses per hour, 10 MB / 50,000-line upload limit, report shown in the browser.
  • Accounts are optional and unlock premium capabilities. See Credits for what credits cost and what they do.

Credits

  • Credits unlock premium capabilities (for example, PDF export costs 2 credits). Credits are deducted only after a successful operation — failed analyses cost nothing.
  • Credits never expire. Stored reports and PDFs expire 7 days after creation.
  • All sales are final. Credit purchases are non-refundable, subject to applicable legal obligations.

Acceptable use

  • Only upload files you have the right to analyze. Don't try to evade rate limits, quotas, or access controls, and don't abuse the service.
  • We may throttle, suspend, or terminate accounts that abuse the service.

Your content, our handling

  • You keep all rights to the logs you upload. How we handle them is described in Privacy.

As-is service

  • StackSift is provided as-is, without warranties. We aim for reliable, accurate analysis but don't guarantee it — always use your own judgment on production incidents.
  • We may change or discontinue features; these terms may change, and we'll note updates here.
